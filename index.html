iTeaGrow: AI-IoT System For Tea Leaf Monitoring, Fertilization, and Powder Grading

An integrated AI–IoT research platform designed to modernize decision-making in the Sri Lankan tea industry. Replacing subjective, manual assessment with data-driven, explainable, and hybrid-capable intelligence.

Explore Our Research View Presentations

High-Level System Architecture

Disease Monitoring

Automated image-based detection of fungal and nutrient-related diseases, correlated with real-time temperature, humidity, and environmental moisture telemetry.

Yield & Maturity

Addressing inaccuracies in manual grading by integrating vision-based maturity detection with quality-adjusted yield prediction using hybrid ML models.

Soil & Fertilization

Data-driven fertilization planning via multi-depth NPK and pH sensing, aligned with Tea Research Institute (TRI) standards.

Powder Grading

Providing transparent, data-backed tea powder grading and regression-based market valuation to replace subjective auctions.
