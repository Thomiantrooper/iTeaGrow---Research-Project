Research Domain

Developed as part of an undergraduate research initiative focused on applying AI, IoT, and Explainable ML to real-world agricultural decision systems in Sri Lanka.

System Objectives

  • Enable early detection of disease and environmental/soil-related stress.
  • Reduce subjectivity in leaf maturity assessment and yield forecasting to improve harvesting quality.
  • Provide transparent, data-backed tea powder grading and valuation.
  • Support rural deployments through an offline system design.

Key Technical Features

  • Explainable AI (XAI): Grad-CAM heatmaps highlight prediction regions and SHAP verifies tabular forms, improving trust.
  • Offline/Hybrid Design: Inference and logging operate without internet for remote plantations.
  • Multilingual UI: Sinhala, Tamil, and English support.
  • Continuous Learning: Feedback loops enable dataset refinement.

Technology Stack

Hardware & IoT

  • ESP32 & SIM800L GSM module
  • NEO-6M GPS
  • NPK, pH, moisture, and temperature sensors
  • Solar–battery hybrid power system

AI & Machine Learning

  • YOLOv8n
  • Random Forest & XGBoost / CatBoost
  • MobileNetV3/V2 & ShuffleNetV2
  • TensorFlow Lite & OpenCV

Mobile & Backend

  • Frontend: React Native
  • Local DB: SQLite
  • Backend: Node.js & Firebase
  • Cloud & Analytics: GCP & Python (Pandas/Scikit-learn)
