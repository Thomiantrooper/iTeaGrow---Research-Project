Research Domain

An undergraduate research initiative applying AI, IoT, and Explainable ML to the Sri Lankan tea industry.

Core System Components

Disease Identification & Environmental Monitoring

Computer Vision and IoT

Combines real-time IoT sensors with lightweight object detection models to accurately identify diseases under varying field conditions.

  • Primary Model: YOLOv8
  • Validated Accuracy: ~95.00%

Leaf Maturity Analyzer & Usable Yield Prediction

Image Classification and ML

Uses mobile-optimized vision models to distinguish tender leaves from mature ones, integrated with ML regression to forecast short-term usable yield.

  • Vision Models: ShuffleNetV2, MobileNetV2
  • Maturity Accuracy: 97.25%
  • Yield Prediction: XGBoost (R² ≈ 0.77)

Soil Monitoring & Fertilizer Recommendation

IoT and ML

Leverages custom sensor networks and intelligent classification to assess soil health, providing safe, TRI-standard aligned fertilizer advice.

  • Approach: ML Classification & Rule-Overrides
  • Validated Accuracy: ~95.00%

Tea Powder Classification & Market Value Analysis

Image Classification and ML

Replaces subjective manual grading by classifying tea powder via imaging, while predicting market auction prices based on quality and external factors.

  • Vision Model: MobileNet
  • Grading Accuracy: 92.50%
  • Market Value: XGBoost (R² = 0.9693)

Technical Innovations

  • Explainable AI (XAI): Grad-CAM heatmaps for imaging and SHAP values for tabular data ensure full model transparency and farmer trust.
  • Edge ML: Optimized lightweight models (YOLOv8n, MobileNet) enable low-power inference directly on mobile and IoT devices.
  • Hybrid Architecture: Combines offline mobile capabilities with IoT edge storage to function effectively in rural areas with intermittent connectivity.
  • Safety-Based Overrides: Hybrid AI systems use domain-informed rules (e.g., TRI guidelines) to prevent harmful fertilizer recommendations.

The iTeaGrow Stack

Integration of high-performance hardware, deep learning, and cloud infrastructure.

Hardware & IoT

  • ESP32 & Multi-sensor Environmental Telemetry
  • MQTT Protocol for low-bandwidth messaging
  • NPK/pH & Soil Moisture Probes
  • Rechargeable Battery Hybrid Edge Devices

Machine Learning Models

  • YOLOv8 (Object Detection)
  • MobileNetV2 & ShuffleNetV2 (Image Classification)
  • XGBoost & Gradient Boosting (Tabular Prediction)
  • Grad-CAM & SHAP (Explainability)

Cloud & Software

  • Frontend: React Native (Mobile-First)
  • Inference: TensorFlow Lite, ONNX
  • Backend: Node.js & Python APIs (Hosted on Railway)
  • Database: MongoDB & SQLite (Edge Sync)
